## 4. Acoustic Architecture & Sound Design Specification

### 4.1. The 3-Tier Audio Mix Standard
All InnoKafe video production assets must strictly adhere to the broadcast **EBU R128 Loudness Standard**:
- **Integrated Loudness**: $-14.0\text{ LUFS} \pm 1.0\text{ LUFS}$
- **True Peak Ceiling**: $\le -1.0\text{ dBTP}$ (preventing DAC clipping across smartphone speakers)
- **Loudness Range (LRA)**: $\le 8.0\text{ LU}$ (preventing overly quiet whisper passages or ear-splitting pops)
- **Audio Sample Rate**: $48,000\text{ Hz}$, $24\text{-bit}$ or $32\text{-bit float}$, AAC-LC $\ge 256\text{ kbps}$ for Web
